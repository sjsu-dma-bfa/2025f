About the Artist

Bryan Ko is an Asian American digital artist from the Bay Area and will soon be graduating with a Bachelor's Fine Arts in Digital Media Art. He first started his art journey in high school, learning photography and videography. At San Jose State University, he’s learned many new skills such as 3D modeling and further enhancing his skills in video editing and Photoshop. His work is inspired by video games and things that he has grown up with. He creates fun and comedic artwork while focusing on self development as an artist. Instead of using his real name in his work, he goes by his artist name, Rya Sprouts or Sprouttess.

Break Point

Break Point explores growth and self-development through playful, game-inspired visuals. Drawing on personal influences and digital media techniques, the work reflects a commitment to learning, iteration, and storytelling through motion and design.
