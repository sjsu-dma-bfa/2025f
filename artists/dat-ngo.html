About the Artist

Dat Ngo is a digital artist studying at San José State University. He first discovered his passion for digital art in high school, where he fell in love with blending code and creativity to bring his ideas to life. His work draws inspiration from pop culture, internet culture, and nostalgia, exploring how technology shapes identity and self-expression. Playful, experimental, and often a bit chaotic, Dat’s art reflects whatever fascinates him in the moment, turning spontaneous curiosity into visual storytelling. He believes the best work happens when creativity is approached with a sense of fun but still treated with genuine care. For Dat, it is about finding the balance between craft and chaos, where technical skill meets imagination and authenticity.

EIRA

Eira is an interactive AI-driven virtual art critic that combines VTuber streaming culture with thoughtful art commentary. This project explores how artificial intelligence, digital performance, and cultural critique can come together to create a virtual personality that delivers art analysis through the lens of internet culture. At its heart, Eira asks whether AI can connect the world of academic art discourse with more open and entertaining forms of conversation. By taking on the voice of a witty and straightforward online streamer, she challenges the formal tone that often surrounds art discussions, turning them into something more engaging and personal. Technically, Eira is built through a layered system that uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the interface, along with a VRM avatar for expressive animations and a Python-based AI for personality-driven dialogue. Voice synthesis and recognition allow for real-time interaction, giving her a lively presence that feels natural. The design contrasts the clean atmosphere of an art gallery with the chaotic energy of online streaming, showing the tension between tradition and the fast pace of digital culture. Beyond the technical side, Eira stands at the crossroads of several modern trends, from the rise of virtual influencers to the growing presence of AI as both an entertainer and a critic. Eira is both a conceptual artwork and a reflection on what it means to share knowledge in the digital age. Her honesty and imperfections question how authenticity is built and what it means to be a credible critic. Whether she is discussing Caravaggio’s dramatic lighting or the visual storytelling in a video game, she treats all creative forms as valuable and worthy of conversation. Through her voice and presence, Eira invites people to think about how expertise, technology, and emotion can come together to shape the way we understand and experience art.
