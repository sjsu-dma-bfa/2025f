Sophia Linarez

About the Artist

Sophia Linarez is an artist belonging to the Bay Area. She has grown up here her entire life and has been able to experience different types of media and culture around her, which she incorporates into her artwork. She likes to incorporate the different inspiration she gets from her favorite films, artists, musicians, video games and from the people in her life. She wants others to see her work and get a sense of nostalgia and invoke a feeling of relatability.

Beatrix’s World

Beatrice’s World is something I first created when I first came to SJSU and took Art 74. She was first inspired by flash 2000s games and the cute bubbly style that you usually see them in and over time I have worked on her design and story. She has grown alongside me here at SJSU and I want to use her for my final art exhibition. My aim is to immerse players to the dangers of escapism and how it isn’t always the answer. I want to explore what it means to be human and how important it is to have connections with those around you and yourself as well. Nowadays it's so easy to shut yourself away and find comfort online and with the many different media on the internet.
